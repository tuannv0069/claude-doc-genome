- decided: Use established task authorization when maintaining triggers and executing a planned change. Ask for clarification when scope or intent is genuinely unresolved rather than requiring a new confirmation for every add-or-skip decision.
- because: The user explicitly authorized autonomous implementation. Requiring ceremonial confirmation after the scope is already established would prevent completion without resolving any uncertainty.
- rejected: Treating an absent reply as approval remains invalid; the authority here comes from the user's actual instructions.
